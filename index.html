Luxembourg-based advisory for European education, establishment and long-term family decisions.

Silver Bay Mountain Group works with international families considering education and family establishment pathways in Luxembourg, Switzerland and selected European markets. We provide structured advisory, institutional coordination and long-term decision support.

Institutional Cooperation Contact
"Good advisory work is not about
recommending answers. It is about helping a family see the shape of what they are choosing."

SBMG was founded in Luxembourg to address a specific gap — the absence of a quiet, institutional advisory firm capable of working with internationally mobile families across the structural decisions that shape long-term outcomes in Europe.

The firm's work spans European education, family establishment, and the longer-arc decisions that often sit between them. We operate from Luxembourg, work in English, French and Chinese, and coordinate with schools, universities and qualified professionals across European jurisdictions.

The firm's posture is intentionally restrained. We do not recruit at scale. We do not promise outcomes. We work with a small number of families and institutions, over time, within clear professional boundaries.

— What we do

Three service lines, one continuous frame of reference.

— 01

European Education Pathways

Advisory and coordination for families considering international schools, career-oriented bachelor programmes, academic pathways and longer-term education planning across Luxembourg, Switzerland and selected European markets.

— 02

Luxembourg Establishment & Family Coordination

Practical coordination for families considering Luxembourg as a long-term European base, including documentation timelines, family-side preparation, school communication, banking coordination and liaison with qualified professional advisers where required.

— 03

Long-Term Family Decision Advisory

Structured advisory for families evaluating education trajectory, country choice, timing, cost, risk and long-term family strategy. The work is designed to support better decisions before irreversible commitments are made.
