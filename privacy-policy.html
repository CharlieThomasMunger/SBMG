Privacy Policy

How Silver Bay Mountain Group handles personal data, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Luxembourg data protection law.

1. Data controller

The data controller is Silver Bay Mountain Group S.à r.l., 8, Rue des Mérovingiens, L-8070 Bertrange, Luxembourg, RCS Luxembourg B293621.

For any data protection request, please contact: contact@sbmg.lu

2. Data we collect

SBMG only collects personal data necessary for the purpose for which it is provided. The categories of data collected may include:

  • Identification data — name, contact details, professional position
  • Family situation data — where provided for the purpose of an advisory engagement
  • Communication data — emails, written correspondence, meeting records
  • Technical data — limited to that necessary for website functionality

3. Purposes of processing

Personal data is processed to respond to enquiries, to provide advisory and coordination services within agreed engagements, to coordinate with institutional counterparts where necessary, and to comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Legal basis for processing

Processing is based on consent (for initial enquiries), performance of a contract (for ongoing engagements), legal obligation where applicable, and legitimate interest limited to those necessary for the firm's ordinary operations.

5. Data sharing

SBMG does not sell personal data. Data is shared only with qualified professional counterparts whose involvement is required for the engagement, institutional counterparts where necessary, service providers acting on behalf of SBMG, and public authorities where required by law.

6. Cookies and technical data

This website uses only the technical cookies strictly necessary for its functioning. No tracking, advertising or analytics cookies are deployed.

7. Data retention

Personal data is retained for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, and in accordance with applicable legal retention obligations (typically up to ten years for commercial documentation under Luxembourg law).

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent. To exercise any of these rights, please contact contact@sbmg.lu.

9. Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Luxembourg data protection authority, the Commission nationale pour la protection des données (CNPD), 15, Boulevard du Jazz, L-4370 Belvaux, Luxembourg.

10. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated date below.

Privacy policy last updated: August 2026.
