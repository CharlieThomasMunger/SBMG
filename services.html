— Services

Three service lines, structured around the decisions families actually face.

SBMG's work is organised around three connected service lines. Each addresses a distinct dimension of the long-term decisions international families navigate in Europe.

I.

European Education Pathways

Advisory and coordination for families considering international schools, career-oriented and academic bachelor programmes, and longer-term education planning across Luxembourg, Switzerland and selected European markets.

Our work supports families through the full arc — from understanding programme positioning and admissions expectations, to preparing realistic questions for institutions, to coordinating the practical steps that follow once a programme is chosen.

  • International schools (primary and secondary)
  • Career-oriented bachelor programmes — hospitality, business, tourism, applied finance, design, culinary arts
  • Academic bachelor and graduate pathways
  • Programme positioning and admissions expectations
  • Documentation and timeline coordination with institutions
  • Long-term education planning across multiple stages
II.

Luxembourg Establishment & Family Coordination

Practical coordination for families considering Luxembourg as a long-term European base, including documentation timelines, family-side preparation, school communication, banking coordination and liaison with qualified professional advisers where required.

The work is structural rather than transactional. Where legal expertise is required, we coordinate with qualified Luxembourg professionals. Our role is to make the family-side process coherent and well-sequenced.

  • Documentation preparation and timeline planning
  • Family-side coordination across institutions
  • School and university liaison during establishment
  • Banking and service provider introductions
  • Coordination with qualified professional advisers as required
  • Practical support during the family's transition into Luxembourg
III.

Long-Term Family Decision Advisory

Structured advisory for families evaluating the dimensions weighed together — education trajectory, country of residence, timing, cost, risk and long-term family strategy. The work is designed to support better decisions before irreversible commitments are made.

This is the work that connects the other two service lines. It is also the work most often skipped — the framing that turns a sequence of separate choices into a coherent long-term direction.

  • Structuring multi-stage education and residence decisions
  • Evaluating trade-offs across countries, programmes and timelines
  • Cost, timing and reversibility analysis
  • Long-term family strategy discussion
  • Pre-decision review before major commitments
  • Ongoing advisory as family circumstances evolve
— Our approach

Each service line operates within the same professional posture.

Advisory rather than transactional. Long-term rather than transactional. Coordination with qualified professionals where regulated expertise is required. Clear professional boundaries throughout. Our Service Principles set this out in full.
