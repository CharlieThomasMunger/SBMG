— About the firm

A Luxembourg advisory firm, working at the intersection of education, establishment and long-term family strategy.

SBMG was founded to address a specific gap — the absence of a quiet, institutional advisory firm capable of working with internationally mobile families on the structural decisions that compound over time.

— The firm

An institutional posture, by design.

Silver Bay Mountain Group operates from Luxembourg, working in English, French and Chinese. The firm's work centres on three connected service lines — European education pathways, Luxembourg establishment, and long-term family decision advisory — and is supported by ongoing institutional dialogue with schools, universities and qualified professional partners across European jurisdictions.

The firm's posture is deliberately restrained. We do not recruit at scale. We work with a small number of families and institutions, over time, within clear professional boundaries.

— Our base in Luxembourg

A small country with predictable institutions.

Luxembourg offers something specific that does not always show up in country comparison tables: a tri-lingual public education system, long-term settlement prospects within a stable EU framework, institutional stability that survives political cycles elsewhere, and a financial centre that rewards substance over presence.

These quiet advantages compound over fifteen years — not over fifteen months. They reward families who treat their European presence as a long-term commitment.

— Founder

James Liu

Founder & Managing Director

James Liu is the Founder of Silver Bay Mountain Group, based in Luxembourg. His work focuses on European education, family establishment and long-term planning decisions for international families.

His background combines two decades of cross-border entrepreneurship and advisory work with wealth management studies at the University of Luxembourg, allowing him to understand both family-level decision-making and the institutional environment in Europe.

— On the mark

A single line for water. That is enough.

The mark of Silver Bay Mountain Group draws on the oldest principle in Chinese cosmology — "Heaven produces water with One" — where a single line is sufficient to signify all of water. The three peaks above echo the firm's identity; the single line of water below completes the meaning of Silver Bay. The mark is intentionally restrained, in keeping with the firm's posture in its advisory work.
