— Contact

Engagements begin with a conversation.

SBMG works with families and institutions by referral and by direct enquiry. The firm responds to written enquiries within a working week.

For families

Families considering an engagement are welcome to write to the firm directly. We ask for a brief introduction to the family's situation and the decisions currently in view. Initial responses are designed to clarify whether SBMG is the appropriate counterpart for the work — not to pursue an engagement that does not fit.

For schools, universities and institutional partners

Institutional enquiries — including campus visit coordination, admissions dialogue and longer-term cooperation discussions — are welcomed. SBMG travels regularly across European education markets and is happy to coordinate institutional meetings during planned visits.
